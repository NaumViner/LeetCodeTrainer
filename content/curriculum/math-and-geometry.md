# Numeric and spatial reasoning

## Overview

Translate the prompt into a small mathematical invariant: coordinate differences, divisibility, modular cycles, or counted cases. State every formula’s assumptions.

## Recognition signals

Coordinates, angles, repeated cycles, large counts, exact divisibility, and results requested modulo a constant.

## Complexity

The formula may be constant time, but preprocessing, sorting points, or iterating digits can dominate.

## Common mistakes and practice

Avoid exact floating-point comparisons and overflow before applying modulo. Practice rotating a matrix, detecting collinear points, and modular exponentiation.
