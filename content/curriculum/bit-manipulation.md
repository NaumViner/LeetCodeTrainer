# Bits as compact state

## Overview

Bits can encode boolean membership and reveal invariants such as XOR cancellation. Explain the representation before using a shortcut.

## Recognition signals

A value appears once, powers of two, subset masks, binary flags, and per-bit counting.

## Complexity

Operations on a fixed-width integer are constant time in the usual interview model; iterating all subsets represented by a mask is still exponential.

## Common mistakes and practice

Watch signed shifts, operator precedence, and language-specific integer width. Practice single number, counting bits, and subset enumeration with masks.
